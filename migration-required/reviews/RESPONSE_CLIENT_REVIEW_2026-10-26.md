# Response to the client review of 26 October 2026

Branch `review-fixes-2026-10`. Two migrations: `0034_valuation_submitted_at`, `0035_photo_location` (additive, nullable). Run `manage.py migrate` on deploy.

| # | Review point | What changed | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verifier can edit answers | A verifier corrects their own track's answers (General also the unassigned questions) while the valuation is submitted and the track is not yet verified. `PUT /api/verifications/<id>/<track>/answers/`, same validation as the assessor's save, audited as `verifier_correct_<track>`. Documents stay read-only. | `apps/valuations/verification.py`, `services.save_section(statuses=…)`, `frontend/src/verifications.jsx` |
| 2 | Date of inspection repeated in Legal/General/Social | The cause was questions with no track (e.g. Date of inspection on L & B (SBIN/CNRB), Land (CNRB)), which were shown in every track. They now show only under General. | `verification.track_questions` |
| 3 | Eligible / desired loan amount not mandatory | Optional; blank is stored as NULL (not zero). | `apps/valuations/submission.py`, Submission form |
| 4 | "Are you sure you want to submit the report?" | App modal before valuation submit, bank report generate/regenerate, and final approval. | `valuations.jsx`, `bankreports.jsx`, `approvals.jsx` |
| 5 | Submission date and time, 24-hour | New `Valuation.submitted_at`, set on submit and shown as `D MMM YYYY, HH:mm` in the submission tab and the verification, bank-report and approval queues. Every displayed timestamp is now 24-hour (`lib/format.dateTime`, `hour12: false`, MIS export header). Imported valuations have no time and show their date. | `submission.py`, `lib/format.js` |
| 6 | Next window after verification | After verifying, the same valuation's next unverified track opens: Legal → Social → Technical → General. After the last one, the queue returns. API returns `next_track`. | `verification.next_track`, `verifications.jsx` |
| 7 | Auto-fill bank, branch, IFSC, ERV, FMV, DSV | Bank and branch were already filled in from the office. The branch can now also be found from the office IFSC, and the IFSC is shown. FMV/ERV/DSV are prefilled from the valuation's answers ("Other Market Value" FMV/ERV/DSV table, else written value questions; "after completion" and "valued earlier" are ignored). They remain editable and the source is named. On a sample of 400 imported reports, about 97% matched the figures that were finally reported; the misses are mostly figures typed in lakhs. | `apps/reports/autofill.py`, `reports/services.report_json`, `bankreports.jsx` |
| 8 | Photos on site | "Take photo" opens the phone camera (`capture="environment"`). The photo records position, accuracy and capture time (`ValuationAttachment.latitude/longitude/location_accuracy/captured_at`), and the time and coordinates are stamped on the image, so they print in the report. Photos chosen from files carry no location. Needs HTTPS and location permission. | `apps/valuations/files.py`, `valuations.jsx` Photos |
| 9 | Discount head in billing | Valuation billing already had "Discount (if any)". The invoice screen now has a Discount field, prefilled from the valuation. It replaces the valuation's discount on the invoice, is always a reduction, can't exceed the charges, and is written back to the valuation's discount charge. | `apps/billing/services.py`, `invoices.jsx` |
